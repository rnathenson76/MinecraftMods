The numbers Minecraft actually gets

 ScrawnyBuff

Minecraft measures models in units, and 16 units = 1 block. The scale row is the number that goes in the behaviour pack — the two models are different shapes, so the scale values aren't 15× apart even though the squirrel is. Height is what's 15×, tail not counted. Turn on Hitbox to see the box he actually occupies: much smaller than he looks, which is why a 7½-block squirrel still fits through your world.
